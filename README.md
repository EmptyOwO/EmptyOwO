## ☾・about me

Hi, I'm **EmptyOwO**!

I'm an introverted person who enjoys drawing and fictional stories,
interesting characters, and stories that leave me thinking
about them long after I've finished reading or watching.

I'm a little awkward sometimes, but feel free to interact
with me! I don't mind meeting people who share similar
interests, and you're welcome to tell me about the things
